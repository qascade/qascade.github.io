I'm Shubh Karman Singh. I write software, and I keep notes while I study.

This site is the readable version of my
[second-brain](https://github.com/qascade/second-brain) repository — the notes
I take while working through the **Bhagavad Gita** and the **Mahabharata**,
one chapter at a time.

## What these notes are

Paraphrases in my own words, written to check whether I actually understood
something. They include my own questions and the places I'm still stuck —
see [my open questions](/gita-my-open-questions/) for the clearest example.

## What they are not

They are not a translation, and not teaching. I am a student of these texts,
not an authority on them. Where I quote a published translation I say whose it
is, and I quote only enough to discuss it. For the texts themselves, go to the
originals and to people who have spent their lives on them.

## Sources I'm learning from

Much of my understanding — and several whole pages of these notes — comes from
other people's lectures and commentaries. Credit where it's due:

- **Ami Ganatra's** lectures and writing on the Gita and the Mahabharata.
- **Premanand ji's** discourses.
- Published translations, including **Srila Prabhupada's**, which I sometimes
  compare against to see how much a translation shapes the meaning.

Any errors in these notes are mine, not theirs. If you spot one, the
[repository](https://github.com/qascade/second-brain) is the place to tell me.
